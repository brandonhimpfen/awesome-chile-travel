> A curated list of resources for traveling in Chile, covering official portals, transport, region guides, national parks, and remote work resources.

Chile is a dramatic South American destination stretching along a narrow strip between the towering Andes mountains and the Pacific Ocean, encompassing the hyper-arid Atacama Desert and Patagonian fjords. This list is intended for alpine hikers, stargazing enthusiasts, outdoor adventurers, and nature conservationists.

## Official & Planning

Essential official portals, migration services, park reservations, and meteorological tracking for Chile.

- [Chile Travel (Sernatur)](https://chile.travel/en/) - Official tourism board portal detailing regional destinations, entry updates, and activity guides.
- [Servicio Nacional de Migraciones](https://serviciomigraciones.cl/en/home/) - Official immigration portal for entry permits, tourist visas, and border requirements.
- [CONAF - National Forest Corporation](https://www.conaf.cl/) - Federal management authority for Chilean national parks, reserves, and natural monuments.
- [Pases Parques](https://www.pasesparques.cl/) - Official online reservation portal to purchase advance entry passes for national parks across Chile.
- [MeteoChile](https://www.meteochile.gob.cl/) - Official National Meteorological Directorate offering regional weather forecasts and climate advisories.

## Transportation

Navigating long-haul bus routes, domestic airlines, regional transit cards, and ferry systems.

- [Recorrido.cl](https://www.recorrido.cl/) - Major platform for searching, comparing, and booking long-distance intercity buses throughout Chile.
- [Bip! Card (Red Movilidad)](https://www.red.cl/) - Official transit card system for navigating Santiago's metro network, buses, and suburban trains.
- [LATAM Airlines Chile](https://www.latamairlines.com/cl/es) - National flagship carrier providing high-frequency domestic flights between Santiago and regional hubs.
- [SKY Airline](https://www.skyairline.com/chile) - Chilean low-cost domestic carrier operating major routes north and south.
- [JetSMART Chile](https://jetsmart.com/cl/es/) - Ultra-low-cost domestic airline servicing capital cities and regional airports.
- [Naviera Austral](https://www.navieraaustral.cl/) - Essential ferry route network serving the Chilean fjords, Chiloé, and Northern Patagonia coastal points.
- [TABSA (Tierra del Fuego Ferries)](https://www.tabsa.cl/) - Ferry operator providing maritime transport across the Strait of Magellan and Fireland channels.

## Destinations & Regions

### Santiago & Central Valley

- [Santiago Turismo](https://www.santiagoturismo.cl/) - Official municipal tourism site for Santiago, detailing cultural districts, historic sites, and urban parks.
- [Metro de Santiago](https://www.metro.cl/) - Official subway guide, network map, and trip planner for the capital city.

### Atacama & North

- [San Pedro de Atacama Tourism](https://www.sanpedroatacama.org/) - Guide to exploring the high-altitude desert, salt flats, geysers, and astronomical observatories.
- [Arica y Parinacota Travel](https://www.arica.cl/) - Visitor guide for Northern coastal border regions, Lauca National Park, and ancient Chinchorro mummies.

### Valparaíso & Coast

- [Valparaíso Travel Guide](https://www.vlpo.cl/) - Portal for exploring the UNESCO-listed historic port city, funicular elevators, and street art hills.

### Lakes & Volcanoes

- [Pucón Tourism](https://www.chile.travel/en/where-to-go/forests-lakes-and-volcanoes/pucon/) - Official region hub for adventure sports, Villarrica Volcano treks, and thermal hot springs.
- [Chiloé Island Guide](https://www.chiloe.cl/) - Cultural travel portal for exploring wooden churches, stilt houses (palafitos), and island traditions.

### Patagonia & Carretera Austral

- [Torres del Paine National Park Guide](https://www.torresdelpaine.cl/) - Visitor portal for trekking the W Circuit and O Circuit in Southern Patagonia.
- [Carretera Austral Official Guide](https://www.carreteraaustral.cl/) - Comprehensive road trip planner for driving Chile's iconic Southern Highway through Aysén.

### Rapa Nui (Easter Island)

- [Rapa Nui National Park (CONAF)](https://www.conaf.cl/parques/parque-nacional-rapa-nui/) - Official park entrance rules, accredited guide requirements, and Moai site conservation protocols.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers, remote workers, and extended stays.

- [InvestChile - Nomad & Tech Visas](https://investchile.gob.cl/) - Government economic agency providing guidance on foreign worker entry, tech startup visas, and residency pathways.
- [Santiago Remote Work Hubs](https://www.startupchile.org/) - Innovation and incubator platform hosting coworking resources and community events in Santiago.

## Safety & Nature Regulations

Practical recommendations on high-altitude travel, volcanic alerts, seismic safety, and leave-no-trace outdoor rules.

- [SENAPRED (National Disaster Prevention Authority)](https://senapred.cl/) - Official emergency agency broadcasting real-time alerts on seismic, tsunami, and volcanic activity.
- [SAG Entry Requirements (Agricultural Inspection)](https://www.sag.gob.cl/) - Official agricultural clearance procedures for declaring organic goods and food items at all Chilean border crossings.
